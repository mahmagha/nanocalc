# A treatise of references for nanomaterials

_A curated, annotated and verified bibliography for nanomaterials synthesis, characterization and data analysis. It is generated by `tools/build-treatise.js` from `js/library.js`; do not edit it by hand._

**220 works in 15 topics.** All 113 works cited by NanoCalc's calculators are included, together with 107 further-reading works chosen to cover the field. Every entry says what it is good for. Hand-annotated entries come first in each list; entries marked _(source for …)_ are placed by the calculator that cites them.

## How the references were verified

Each entry was checked by `tools/verify-refs.js`; results are in `docs/references/verification.json`:

- **Articles and books with a DOI:** the DOI was resolved in Crossref. Title words, first-author surname and year (±1, online vs print) must match, and volume and first page are compared when known.
- **Works without a DOI:** Crossref was searched (books also on Open Library, which confirms title and author; editions and years are as cited and may differ from the first edition). A hit is accepted only if it is the same kind of work and matches title, author and year; for articles it must also match volume and first page. This rule was tightened after a looser version paired a 1947 article with a 1948 book of the same name.
- **Standards** (ISO, JCGM, EU) cannot be checked through Crossref. Each was looked up on the issuing body's site on 2026-11-16 to confirm it is the current edition. Three withdrawn editions were replaced this way: ISO 19430:2016 → 2024, ISO 13099-2:2012 → 2025 and ISO 22412:2017 → 2025. ISO/TS 80004-2:2015 was dropped because it is superseded by ISO 80004-1:2023.

## Appendix B: adding a reference

1. Add the work to `js/library.js`: further reading as a full entry, or a calculator citation via `NC.addRefs` in the calculator file plus a `{ ref }` entry in the library. Include a DOI only if you are certain of it; the verifier finds missing ones.
2. Run `make verify-refs` (needs network). Fix any mismatch; never keep an entry that fails.
3. For a standard, confirm the current edition on the issuing body's website and add its catalogue `url`.
4. Run `make treatise` and `make check`, then commit the library, `docs/references/verification.json` and this file together.
